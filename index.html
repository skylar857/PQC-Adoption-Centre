    The study at a glance

    Question

    How many HTTPS servers already negotiate the hybrid group X25519MLKEM768, and does adoption differ between sectors?

    Method

    One TLS 1.3 handshake per host with OpenSSL 3.5, offering the hybrid first and X25519 as fallback. Control handshakes separate real non-support from failed connections.

    Scope

    A hand-selected sample of Indian sites plus the global top list. Not a random sample, so results describe this sample only.
